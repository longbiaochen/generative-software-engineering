# Sub-agent handoff

- **Result:** What the sub-agent must produce.
- **Baseline:** Repository/state/commit and facts shared with the root GSE.
- **Scope:** Read scope and exclusive write scope, if any.
- **Constraints:** Dependencies, authorization boundaries, or resources that matter.
- **Evidence:** What observations or artifacts must accompany the result.
- **Return:** Concise conclusion, changed artifacts, validation, and unresolved blockers.
